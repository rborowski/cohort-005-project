# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the people who run the platform and the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of the Cadence staff who runs the platform as a whole, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), Staff, Superuser

**Team Admin**:
A person who manages a team that bought seats, such as handing out its coupon links. It gives no power over the platform.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), Team owner, Manager

### Purchases and access

**Purchase**:
One payment for a course. Every Purchase is either a Personal Purchase or a Team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Personal Purchase**:
A Purchase someone makes for themselves, which gives them an Enrollment.

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase of one or more Seats for a team to hand out. It gives the buyer no Enrollment of their own.
_Avoid_: Team pack, Licence, Bulk buy

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Purchase, handed out as a coupon link. A Seat is Claimed once a learner redeems it, and Unclaimed until then.
_Avoid_: Licence, Coupon (for the place itself)

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. Approving their Refund Request ends it.
_Avoid_: Access, Registration, Membership

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
