# Refunded learners keep their progress

Approving a Refund Request ends the learner's Enrollment but deliberately keeps their lesson progress, watch events and quiz attempts. Deleting them can't be undone. The watch events are the evidence behind the 2-hour cap if a Refund is ever disputed. And a learner who buys the course again expects to pick up where they left off. Please don't add a "clean-up" step that deletes this data on refund. If data-protection rules ever require deleting it, that's a separate decision to make in its own right.

## Consequences

- Progress belongs to the learner and the lesson, not to the Enrollment, so it comes back by itself if they buy again.
- A learner can buy the same course again, but only one Refund Request per learner per course can ever be approved. Without this rule, someone could keep buying, watching up to the cap, and getting their money back.
