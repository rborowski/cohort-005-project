# Access ends when a Refund Request is approved, not when the Refund is issued

A learner keeps full access to a course while their Refund Request is Open, and loses it the moment a Platform Admin approves it. We don't wait until the Refund is Issued. Freezing access as soon as someone asks would punish learners whose request is later declined, and the 2-hour cap already limits what they can watch for free. Waiting for the money to be confirmed would tie access to a payment step we don't control, and the glossary already says a Failed Refund leaves the approval standing. Approval is the one point where the team's decision is final.

## Consequences

- An Open request can't be used to get around the cap: anything watched while it's Open shows on the Platform Admin's screen.
- A learner can be without access before their money has actually come back. The approval email has to say so plainly.
