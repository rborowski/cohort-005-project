# Refund Requests cover Personal Purchases only, for now

A learner can raise a Refund Request only on their own Personal Purchase. A learner on a Claimed Seat can't, because the Seat belongs to the team that bought it, and they're told to ask their Team Admin. Refunds on Team Purchases are on hold until they have their own entry point. Before this, the course page refunded whichever Purchase was most recent, so a buyer who owned both kinds could ask for a Refund on the whole Team Purchase without meaning to.

## Consequences

- Team Purchase Refund Requests raised before this change still show on the Platform Admin's screen, but they can only be declined.
- Things to keep when Team Purchase Refunds are built:
  - A Refund is always the full amount, so a Team Purchase with any Claimed Seat can't be refunded. There are no partial refunds for the Unclaimed Seats.
  - If a Seat is claimed while the request is Open, approval is blocked rather than claiming being frozen.
  - Approving should stop every Unclaimed Seat's coupon link from working.
