# Only Platform Admins decide Refund Requests

The owning instructor counts as "staff" for their course everywhere else in the app (`isStaff`), but they can't see or decide Refund Requests. Only Platform Admins can. Refunds are about Cadence's money and its promises to learners, not about course content, and an instructor has a conflict of interest when deciding refunds on their own course. Team Admins have no say either, since that role gives no power over the platform.
